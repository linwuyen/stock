# Model Assumption Policy

1. Reported facts and valuation assumptions are separate fields.
2. Bear/Base/Bull EPS and PE multiples are model inputs, never presented as company guidance unless an evidence record explicitly proves otherwise.
3. A candidate must beat the TSMC benchmark by the configured Alpha Spread and MOS gates; a high standalone score is insufficient.
4. Missing required freshness dates fail the Buy Gate.
5. A stale or incomplete TSMC benchmark blocks rotation just like a stale candidate.
6. No model output creates or sends a trade.
